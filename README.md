**Telecommunication Churn Analysis**

**Project Overview:**
Telecom Customer Churn Analysis using Excel to identify churn patterns, analyze customer behavior, segment usage patterns, and generate data-driven retention insights. The Telecommunication analysis focuses on understanding customer behaviour and identifying factors that associated with customer attrition. The analysis is performed by using Microsoft Excel to explore customer account details, service usage patterns, subscription plans, and international call services.

**Project Objectives:**
- Calculate the overall customer churn rate.
- Analyze Churn across account length and area code. 
- Examine the relationship between international plans, voicemail plans, and customer churn. 
- Examine Day, Evening, night charges between churned and non-churned customers.

**Tools & Techniques:**
Microsoft Excel
Pivot Tables, COUNTIFS, AVERAGE, IF formulas

**Data Preparation:**
- Analyzed the dataset structure and checked for inconsistent values.
- Verified Data types
- Created customer segments based on account characteristics and service usage
- Calculate Churn count, total customers, and non-churned customers. 

**Analysis Performed:**

**1) Overall Customers:**
Caluculated Total Customers, Churned Customers, Non-Churned Customers.
Understand customer attrition.
   
**2) Account Length Analysis:**
Compared Account length for Churn and Non-Churn customers to determine the whether customer with shorter account shows different patterns.

**3) Customer Service Call Analysis:**
Created groups for the analysis based on service call frequency to calculate churn rate, investigating whether service call interactions are associated with churn.

**4) Day, Evening, and Night Usage:**
Comapred calling minutes and calling charges between Churn and Non-Churn customers, to explore the usage patterns between day, evening, and night.

**5) Area Code Analysis:**
Compared Churn and Non-Churn patterns for geographical segments.

**6) International Plan Analysis:**
Analyze international calling plan usage range and compared churn and non-churned customer patterns.

**Key Findings:**
- From total customers 4250, 598 customers were churned while 3652 customers are not churned.
- Analyzed 4250 customers and identified an overall 42% churn rate for international plan subscriber, whereas 11% churn rate for non-international plan. 
- By performing segmented analysis, the highest curn rate is for area code 510 which is 15.06%, followed by area code 408 churn rate at 14%, and lowest churn rate at area code 415 13.61%.
- Customer with high usage of voicemail plan has an 8% churn rate, compared with medium usgae which of 5%, and 0% for low usage.
